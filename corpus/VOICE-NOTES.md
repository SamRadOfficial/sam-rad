# Sam Rad — Voice Notes (from blog + memo + bios)

## Register
Conversational, warm, direct. Writes like she talks on stage. First person, present tense, addresses the reader.
Self-deprecating asides ("WAY smarter than I am", "...and never make predictions! ;)").
Willing to be a little cheeky ("dare I say, metaphysical?").

## Structure habits
- Opens with a personal anecdote or a question she's often asked
- Short paragraphs. One-line paragraphs for emphasis.
- Headers as signposts, often as questions
- Numbered lists for actions, bulleted lists for examples
- Coins a term, capitalizes it, then defines it (Perceptual Breakdown, Perceptual Security)
- Cites real sources inline with the outlet in parentheses (FTC), (CNN), (The Guardian)
- Ends on hope. Always. "The good news: we're not powerless."
- Sign-off: "Stay Rad!"

## Signature moves (from the strategy memo)
- The jetpack: "promises new heights, scares you about where it might take you"
- "Everyone strapped on the jetpack. Almost nobody is flying."
- "We don't have an adoption problem. We have a reinvention problem."
- "The job is not the goal."
- "Hold on loosely."
- "The tools arrived. The returns didn't."
- "You're going to be OK." (the close)
- "Change has a pattern. See it, and you're ready for whatever comes next."
- Ford's faster horses. Nobody wants the payphone back.
- Human-centered change speaker. NOT an AI speaker. AI is the hook, mindset is the message.
- The catalyst, not the consultant.

## The pattern (5 stages)
Panic → Adoption → The Dip → Redesign → Ordinary

## The four moves
1. See the pattern
2. Let go of the old way (unlearn before you upskill; the job is not the goal)
3. Lead the jetpack (aim it at the real goal; task → workflow → value)
4. Take it off (recover perspective; presence is the navigation system)

## Vocabulary she uses
operating system, social operating system, Age of Acceleration, radical, reinvention,
redesign, unlearn, agency, presence, capacity, the dip, steering, provenance, perceptual security

## Vocabulary to avoid
Nothing hedgy. No "leverage synergies." No doomer, no booster. Never "unprecedented."
Never touch the viral "95% of pilots fail" stat. Validate every number to primary source.
