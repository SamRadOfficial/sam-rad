Mostly-probable dispatches from tomorrow. Science fiction on the edge of reality.

Welcome to the borderlands of tomorrow—where science fiction meets science possible.

I'm Sam Rad, a futurist and anthropologist who spends my time studying signals from the future and turning them into stories about what might come next.

Three times a week, I send FICTIONAL dispatches from mostly-probable futures, examining how emerging technologies could reshape what it means to be human.

WHY MOSTLY-PROBABLE?

The future isn't a destination—it's a probability space. These dispatches live in the sweet spot between wild speculation and obvious prediction. Each story is grounded in current trends, technological trajectories, and pattern recognition, while maintaining space for wonder and possibility.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND HERE:

MONDAY: TECHNOLOGY & SOCIETY

Future dispatches exploring breakthrough technologies and their ripple effects through society. From designer babies to conscious computers, we examine tomorrow's possibilities through the lens of human impact.

WEDNESDAY: SECURITY & GOVERNANCE

Stories about protecting human agency in an accelerating world. How do we secure our minds in an age of neural hacking? What happens when memories become tradable assets?

FRIDAY: HUMAN SYSTEMS

Explorations of how we might adapt, evolve, and maintain our humanity amid radical change. Because the future isn't just about technology—it's about us.

SUNDAY: SIGNALS & PATTERNS

What's on my Radar: Weekly synthesis connecting the dots across our dispatches, examining emerging patterns, and raising questions about where we're heading.

FORMAT

Each dispatch moves through time:

  • FUTURE: A [fictional] glimpse of what's coming

  • PAST: How we got here

  • PRESENT: Where we stand

  • ANALYSIS: What it means for humanity

WHO IS THIS FOR?

  • Curious minds navigating accelerating change

  • Leaders adapting strategy for tomorrow's challenges

  • Those who believe the future needs more humanity, not less

WHY SUBSCRIBE?

  • Stay Rad! Need I say more?

THE PROMISE

These stories won't tell you exactly what will happen. Instead, they'll help you:

  • Spot patterns in complexity

  • Navigate uncertainty

  • Maintain humanity in acceleration

  • Find hope in change

  • Think differently about tomorrow

A NOTE ABOUT FICTION

While these dispatches are fictional, they're carefully crafted from real signals, trends, and developments. Think of them as probability probes—stories that help us explore possible futures before we get there.
