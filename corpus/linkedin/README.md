# LinkedIn export

Exported by Sam on 14 September 2026 via Settings, Data Privacy, Get a copy of your
data. All posts are `MEMBER_NETWORK` visibility, which is public; nothing here was
connections-only.

- `posts.csv`: 904 posts, 2013 to 2026, about 94,000 words. Columns: Date, ShareLink,
  ShareCommentary (the text), SharedUrl, MediaUrl, Visibility.
- `articles/`: 53 long-form LinkedIn articles as exported HTML.

## Read this before using it for voice

The corpus has two Sams in it and they are not the same writer.

| years | posts | who was writing |
|---|---|---|
| 2017 to 2019 | 326 | Chronicled-era Sam: blockchain, IoT, enterprise. Denser, more technical, more "we". |
| 2023 to 2026 | 485 | Current Sam: keynote, futurist, perceptual security. Shorter lines, first person, ends on hope. |
| 2020 to 2022 | 58 | The gap. NYOUM and the pandemic. Thin. |

**Weight the 2023 to 2026 material.** The 2017 to 2019 voice is a useful record of the
subject matter and of how she argues, but it is not the register the site or the
keynote uses now. A voice guide trained evenly across both will average them into
someone who does not exist.

This folder is not built or deployed. It is source material for `../VOICE.md` once that
exists, and for the dispatch backfill on `/writing`.
